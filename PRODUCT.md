# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Casual puzzle players on mobile and desktop browsers — people who want a quick mental workout without distraction. They play during commutes, breaks, or wind-down time. They expect the app to feel polished, fast, and focused.

## Product Purpose

A distraction-free, ad-free puzzle game hub with three games: Sudoku, Word Search, and Jigsaw. Each game has multiple difficulty levels. Success looks like a player coming back daily because the experience is clean and the games feel satisfying.

## Brand Personality

Focused · Artistic · Playful

## Anti-references

- Busy mobile game UIs full of banners, lives, coins, and reward prompts
- Pure flat white/gray UIs that feel utilitarian and joyless
- Dark-mode-only tools that feel developer-facing, not welcoming

## Design Principles

1. **One color per game** — Each game has a distinct color identity that carries through from the select screen to every game screen. No mixing.
2. **Zero noise** — No ads, no badges, no push notifications, no upsell. The game is the whole screen.
3. **Touch-first** — Every interaction is designed for thumbs first, then mouse. Tap targets are generous.
4. **Artistic without being decorative** — Colors and backgrounds should feel curated, not slapped on. Warm cream for the lobby; saturated immersive tones for each game.
5. **Instant feedback** — Selection, completion, and error states are immediate and satisfying.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Readable contrast throughout (minimum WCAG AA)
- Touch targets minimum 44pt
- No reliance on color alone to convey state
